
- A forensic analyst has a thorough understanding of common operating systems, applications, and devices in the network.
- The analyst focuses on the files that are left behind, because these items can be recorded and analyzed.
- Formal investigations may require maintaining a chain of custody for all evidence and interacting with law enforcement.

- Chain of custody refers to the documentation or paper trail showing the seizure, custody, control, transfer, analysis, and disposition of physical or electronic evidence.
- It is essential in legal contexts to maintain a chain of custody to ensure the validity of evidence.
- John Petruzzi, director of enterprise security at Constellation Energy, emphasizes the importance of dealing with evidence as if it would go to litigation.
- ==Without a chain of custody, evidence may be deemed worthless in legal proceedings.==

- A chain of custody is a ==document that describes the exact time that each person took possession of specific evidence==.
- Investigators should maintain a chain of custody, beginning with when the images are created.
- The custodian is responsible for storing the material in a location that no one else has access to.
- The chain-of-custody process ensures that no one other than the single named custodian had a chance to access or alter the information.
- Evidence often is inadmissible in court if the defense can introduce doubt that only one person at a time had access to the data.
- Investigators should maintain separate documentation for themselves.

Provides such answers to:
- What is the evidence?
- What method was used to collect the evidence?
- When was the evidence collected?
- Who has handled the evidence and why did that person need to handle the evidence?
- Where is the evidence permanently stored?

- The issue of the digital chain of custody becomes important with the increasing cybercriminal activities.
- Digital evidence is different from physical evidence.
- A complete bit-by-bit image copy of a hard drive is as good as the original hard drive.
- The first image of a hard drive that investigators take is known as the “best evidence.”
- The chain of custody form should be attached to the best evidence and stored under lock and key.
- After the best evidence is gathered, a second copy should be made, either from the original or from the best evidence, creating the working copy that investigators use for their research.
- The working copy of the chain of custody form should also be attached to the best evidence and securely stored.
