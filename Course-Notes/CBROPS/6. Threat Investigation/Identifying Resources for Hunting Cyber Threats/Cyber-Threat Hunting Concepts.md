- ==Intrusion Detection Limitations==: Even with advanced security systems, intrusion attempts cannot be detected 100% of the time due to the ever-evolving nature of cyber threats.
- ==Threat Actor Adaptability==: Cyber threats constantly adapt to new detection methods, making it a constant struggle for defenders to stay ahead.
- ==Undetected Intrusions==: It is assumed that somewhere in the network, an undetected intrusion has already occurred, and threat actors may have a foothold.

- ==Cyber Threat Hunt Objective==: Assume network compromise and search for signs of entry to prevent malicious actions.
- ==Security Guard Analogy==: Similar to a security guard patrolling an office building, ensuring security measures are functioning and no unauthorized access has occurred.
- ==Security Measures==: Includes security alarms, locks, fences, and network security protocols.

- ==Threat Detection Method==: Proactively detect malicious activity not identified by traditional alerting mechanisms.
- ==Threat Actor’s Evasion==: Threat actors may evade antivirus and IPS definitions, but their activity can be detected through tell-tale signs.
- ==Data Analysis==: Analyze authentication logs, event data, and traffic flow to correlate data and identify potential threats.
