%%Managing a playbook using a tracking system.%%
![1](/Course-Notes/.assets/Pasted_image_20241202134343.png)

Playbook management includes the following:
- Create a custom field.
- Track the play progress and life cycle.
- Provide basic notification (such as email and RSS).
- Run queuing and assignment functions.
- Automate reports and metrics.
- Document and log changes.

- Security event monitoring is a constantly changing practice.
- New and relevant plays must be developed continually.
- These plays must be managed in a play management system.
- The play management system ensures that the organization is protected from future threats.
