- CSIRT Definition: A service organization responsible for ==receiving, reviewing, and responding to computer security incident reports and activity.==
- CSIRT services are usually performed for a defined constituency that can be a parent entity such as a corporate, governmental, or educational organization; a region or country; a research network; or a paid client.
- CSIRT Structure: Can be a team ==dedicated to incident response== as their major job function ==or an ad hoc team== called together during an incident.
- CSIRT Knowledge: Should have an ==in-depth understanding of the network and its assets== to effectively respond to attacks.

>CSIRT Mission: Ensure company, system, and data preservation by investigating security incidents and preventing them through threat assessment, detection, mitigation planning, incident trend analysis, and security architecture review.

>CSIRT Role in Incident Response: Engages in threat assessment, detection, mitigation planning, incident trend analysis, and security architecture review to protect company, system, and data.

- SOC Role: Identify threats, enact incident response policies, diagnose incidents, and contain them.

CSIRTs come in all shapes and sizes and serve diverse constituencies. Some general categories of CSIRTs include, but are not limited to, the following:

| Type                 | Description                                                                                 | Examples                                                                        |
| -------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Internal CSIRTs      | Provide incident handling services to their parent organization.                            | Bank CSIRT, Manufacturing Company CSIRT, University CSIRT, Federal Agency CSIRT |
| National CSIRTs      | Provide incident handling services to a country.                                            | JPCERT/CC, Singapore Computer Emergency Response Team (SingCERT)                |
| Coordination Centers | Coordinate and facilitate incident handling across various CSIRTs.                          | CERT Coordination Center, US-CERT                                               |
| Analysis Centers     | Synthesize data from various sources to determine trends and patterns in incident activity. | N/A                                                                             |
| Vendor Teams         | Handle reports of vulnerabilities in their software or hardware products.                   | Cisco Product Security Incident Response Team (PSIRT)                           |
